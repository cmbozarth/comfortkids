Comfort Kids Doll Ministry



Who are Comfort Kids?

Comfort Kids are handmade dolls designed to be huggable and cute. They're given to support children who are facing difficult times in their lives.

Get Involved!

We are always looking for helpers, from sewing to embroidering to helping stuff dolls. Learn more about how you can get involved in this doll ministry.

FAQ

Comfort Kids are given to children of different ages and circumstances, so our priority is making sure the recipient feels safe and is safe. Read our FAQ for more information.
