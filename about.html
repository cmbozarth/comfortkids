About Comfort Kids

Comfort Kids Doll Ministry

Comfort Kids are 100% handmade dolls designed to be huggable and cute. They are unique and contemporary for kids to hug, play with, and be comforted.

Comfort Kids have been donated to DSS, Women's Shelters, and to children and families that are facing difficult scenarios. Children going through an abrupt transition, such as transitioning to foster care, often cannot take their toys and personal items for various reasons, such as for health and safety.

This transition can be stressful, so Comfort Kids seeks to help ease this transition and bring hope to the children while they adjust to their new home. A friendly face, outstretched arms, and a well-stuffed huggable body can give the child something to cling to in times of stress.
